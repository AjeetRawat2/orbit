    How the film is made

    1. Claude writes a treatment as scenes: one idea per scene, a hook, one turn, an ending that echoes the start.
    2. The page turns each scene into hand-wobbled strokes. Every frame is a pure function of time, so you can scrub anywhere.
    3. The camera travels across one large board. The last shot pulls back to show the whole lesson at once.
    4. Pen sounds, a small score and the narration are generated live in your browser.

    Method, guides and styles from lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar (code and docs MIT; demo films CC BY 4.0, credit LemoLab). This page draws its own films and uses none of the library's demo artwork. Narration uses your browser's built-in voices and is not part of a saved file.
