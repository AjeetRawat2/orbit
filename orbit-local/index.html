      How the film is made

      1. Gemini casts the characters and writes the scenes: a hook, one turn where the music drops out, and an ending that echoes the start.
      2. The page animates a rigged cast (a robot host, people, blobs for cells and atoms) and about forty moving objects.
      3. The director picks one of 60 looks from the Lemo-Opuscar library to fit the topic (a blueprint for machines, a microscope for cells, ink wash for calm, a dossier for mysteries), or you choose one from the Style menu and switch it any time.
      4. Every frame is a pure function of time, so you can scrub anywhere. Sound effects and score run in your browser. Narration uses Gnani when configured, with the browser default voice as fallback.

      Method and guides from lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar (code and docs MIT). All characters here are original designs. Choose a film language above; Gnani narration is included in WebM exports when configured.
